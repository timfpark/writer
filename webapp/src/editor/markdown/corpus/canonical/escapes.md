\# Not a heading, just a hash.

\- Not a list item either.

1\. Not an ordered list.

\> Not a quote.

Brackets \[like these\] and a pipe \| and a tilde \~ stay literal.

A snake_case_name stays as typed, and so does a < b.

An ampersand \&amp; reads as text, while AT&T needs nothing.

Backslash \\ and backtick \` are escaped.

&#32;Leading space is kept, and so is trailing space&#32;

Wow\![a link](https://example.com) after an exclamation mark.
